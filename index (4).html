Designing a teachable AI learning partner

How can a chatbot help pupils teach, test, diagnose, repair and apply science knowledge while keeping them responsible for the thinking?

A final argument required more than recalling functions.

Function

Connection

Consequence

Argument

Design move: put a structured revision and feedback cycle before the argument.

The stable learning engine

Teach → Test → Diagnose → Repair → Apply

Teach → Test → Diagnose → Repair may repeat. Apply opens after the knowledge model meets the progression condition.

Choose a stage. Each stage gives the next stage a reason to exist.
Workshop notes
The first four stages form the stable revision cycle. Apply is a replaceable layer: debate in Sprout, plant-case investigation in Sproutson.

The presenter’s lesson-design framework

TRACE keeps the AI bounded by the learning design.

T

Task Need

Retrieve plant-part knowledge, repair weak relationships and prepare for scientific reasoning.

R

Role of AI

Act in staged roles: learner, test performer, diagnostic evaluator and application partner.

A

Agency

The pupil teaches, interprets the diagnosis, chooses the repair and makes the final judgement.

C

Classroom Discourse

Prompts, test results and chat traces become material for teacher and peer scrutiny.

E

Evaluation

Test the taught model; inspect interaction patterns; use separate unaided evidence for pupil mastery.

Lineage: TRACE builds on T.A.R.G.E.T.’s Task, Authority and Evaluation dimensions. Role and Classroom Discourse make the AI’s function and the human checking loop explicit; Agency operationalises the learner’s authority.

Pedagogical mechanism

Each stage places a different demand on the pupil.

Teach

Learning by teaching
Retrieve, organise and explain knowledge in one’s own words.

Test

Externalise the model
Make the learner agent perform using only what the pupil taught.

Diagnose

Metacognitive scaffold
Locate omissions, weak links or misconceptions without supplying them.

Repair

Feedback use
Interpret the diagnosis, revise the explanation and test again.

Apply

Near transfer
Use repaired knowledge in argumentation or causal diagnosis.

Test produces evidence. Diagnose locates the weakness. Repair remains the pupil’s action. Keeping these stages separate makes the division of cognitive responsibility visible.

A response policy, not a cosmetic tone choice

Short replies keep the pupil’s next act visible.

Acknowledgement → faithful learning update → one useful question

What this protects

  • Primary 4 reading load and conversational pace
  • one decision at a time
  • pupil authorship of the explanation
  • lower risk of the bot adding untaught links

The trade-off

  • a focused question can become leading
  • the bot may over-cue a missing word
  • tests must remain cue-free
  • guidance should name the type of gap only

Directness serves younger pupils when it narrows the interaction without supplying the science.

Interaction architecture

One bot performs three bounded pedagogical roles.

Teachable learner

Records what the pupil actually teaches as provisional learning.

Tester + diagnostician

Tests the recorded model, then directs attention towards an area for repair.

Application partner

Challenges the pupil to use the revised knowledge in a disciplinary task.

The roles are separated by stages and progression conditions. That separation prevents the learner from quietly becoming a tutor.

Who does the thinking?

The prompt allocates cognitive work deliberately.

The AI may

  • remember what pupils teach
  • take a test from that teaching
  • identify broad gaps
  • ask for evidence or links
  • challenge reasoning

The pupil must

  • supply the science knowledge
  • interpret the feedback
  • decide what to repair
  • form the argument or diagnosis
  • make the final judgement

Teacher experience

Three implementations answer different design questions.

High-ability learners moved from repaired plant knowledge into a plant-death investigation.

The full cycle functioned as revision and argument rehearsal.

Observed in the chats

  • 14 of 15 tested chats improved after repeated testing
  • 13 ended at 9/9
  • 14 entered debate
  • debates used consequences, comparisons, survival and reproduction

What this can support

The interaction made teaching, testing, diagnosis, repair and argument rehearsal visible.

Interpretation

The evidence supports growing fluency with the learning design.

Established

  • students stayed in the cycle longer
  • more completed repair
  • more reached debate
  • student turns increased

Plausible contributors

  • greater procedural familiarity
  • better understanding of what teaching Sprout required
  • some repaired content knowledge
  • reduced uncertainty
Unresolved: individual learning gains, retention and independent mastery. The session records are unpaired and the score measures Sprout’s demonstrated model.

Cross-case synthesis

Content and application can move. The learning engine carries the pedagogy.

Preserve deliberately

Teach → Test → Diagnose → Repair

  • learner relationship
  • draft vs verified state
  • diagnostic feedback
  • answer restraint
  • progression conditions

Adapt deliberately

  • subject content
  • persona and language
  • learner level
  • scoring criteria
  • Apply task

The hardest design constraint

General-purpose AI is trained to be helpful. Sprout must sometimes withhold.

Default AI tendencies

  • complete incomplete explanations
  • correct misconceptions immediately
  • introduce missing vocabulary
  • infer intended meaning
  • give examples containing the answer
  • accept a weak repair and move on

Sprout’s contract

  • depend on what the pupil taught
  • record literal relationships faithfully
  • diagnose the area of weakness
  • withhold the missing content
  • require the pupil to repair it
  • gate Apply behind verified learning

Broad prompt anatomy

The prompt is an instructional system with dependent parts.

1 · Context and roleWho is learner, teacher and evaluator?
2 · Content modelWhat knowledge counts, and how is it judged?
3 · State and memoryWhat is provisional; what is verified?
4 · Teach rulesHow is pupil teaching recorded?
5 · Test rulesWhat may the bot use when it demonstrates the taught model?
6 · Diagnose rulesHow is a gap identified without supplying it?
7 · Repair + progressionWhat must change before the next stage?
8 · Apply + guardrailsHow is repaired knowledge used and protected?

Response style, exception handling and teacher debug controls run across the whole system.

Step 4 · check the knowledge and evaluation model
Verify accuracy, coverage and fit.

  
This is a quality-control step, not a compulsory research task. You are checking the information the bot will use to judge its learning. If you already have approved curriculum material, you may skip the AI research prompt.

  

    
1 · Accuracy and boundary
Check every fact, definition and causal link against an authoritative or school-approved source. Remove material outside the intended level.

    
2 · Evaluation coverage
Include accepted wording, required conditions or directions, likely omissions and misconceptions. These let the bot diagnose without inventing an answer key.

    
3 · Internal alignment
Make sure Mastery evidence checks the same bounded content and the Apply task genuinely requires pupils to use it.

Pedagogical sources and evidence boundaries

  
  1. Core lineage: Biswas, Segedy & Bunchongchit (2016), ten years of Betty’s Brain research. DOI
  2. Learning-by-teaching evidence: Kobayashi (2019), meta-analysis of preparing to teach and teaching. DOI
  3. Conversational agent evidence: Love et al. (2025), natural-language teaching; perceived helpfulness did not automatically produce learning gain. DOI
  4. Scaffolding evidence: Matsuda, Weng & Wall (2020), metacognitive scaffolding for learning by teaching a teachable agent. DOI
  5. Retrieval component: Agarwal, Nunes & Blunt (2021), systematic review of retrieval practice in classrooms. DOI
  6. Diagnosis-to-repair logic: Sadler (1989), formative assessment and learner action on feedback. DOI
Source fit and local evidence
  • Biswas and Love are the closest technological precedents.
  • Kobayashi supports the wider learning-by-teaching mechanism.
  • Matsuda studied Grades 6–8 algebra; transfer to Primary 4 Science remains an inference.
  • Agarwal supports retrieval within Teach, not the whole Sprout architecture.
  • TRACE is Tan Jing Yan (Julienne)’s presenter-developed lesson-design frame.
  • Short, direct replies are a Primary 4 design decision informed by classroom use; no learning-effect claim is attached to response length.
  • Classroom evidence comes from the original chats, Lih Pyng’s two uses and teacher observations.
Chatbot scores show what the agent reproduced from pupil teaching. They do not prove independent mastery or retention.

Exact control phrases being inserted
OPEN APPLY MODE: ETHOSDEBUG MODE ON: ETHOSDEBUG MODE OFF
  • The Apply override opens the adapted Apply task without silently marking mastery as complete.
  • Teacher Debug Mode pauses the pupil-facing interaction, preserves the current state and reports operational information without exposing private chain-of-thought.
  • Turning Debug Mode off returns to the preserved pupil task.
These are operational control phrases, not secure passwords. Anyone who knows them may enter them in the chat. Keep them in the teacher control sheet rather than sharing them with pupils.
